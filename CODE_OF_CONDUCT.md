# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be direct about the work, generous with context, and respectful of the person on the other side of the screen.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that violate these expectations. Report concerns through the repository's private security contact or to a maintainer on GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as the platform allows.

This is a short project policy, not a substitute for GitHub's Community Guidelines.
